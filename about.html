About FixMaster

Our Story

From a Small Workshop to Chennai's #1 Repair Service

FixMaster was founded in 2009 by Ramesh Krishnan, an ITI-certified electrical engineer who saw a gap in the market for honest, reliable appliance repair. Starting with just a single workshop in Anna Nagar, Chennai, Ramesh focused on two things: technical excellence and customer trust.

Today, FixMaster has grown to a team of 50+ certified technicians serving all of Tamil Nadu. We've repaired over 10,000 appliances and maintained a remarkable 98% customer satisfaction rate — all while keeping our core promise: honest pricing, genuine parts, guaranteed results.

1Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide

  • Contact details: Name, phone number, email address when you book a repair or submit a contact form
  • Address: Your service address when you request home pickup or on-site service
  • Appliance information: Brand, model, and description of your appliance issue
  • Payment information: Processed securely via third-party payment gateways; we do not store card details

Information Collected Automatically

  • Log data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, time on site
  • Cookies: Small text files for session management, analytics, and preference storage (see Cookies section)
  • Device information: Device type, operating system, screen resolution
We only collect the minimum data necessary to provide our repair services effectively.

2How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data solely for legitimate business purposes:

  • To schedule and fulfill your appliance repair booking
  • To communicate repair status updates via WhatsApp and SMS
  • To send service reminders and warranty notifications
  • To improve our website and service quality through analytics
  • To send promotional offers (only with your explicit consent)
  • To comply with legal obligations

Legal basis: Contract performance, legitimate interest, and consent (for marketing).

3Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. We may share data only with:

  • Assigned technicians: Only name and phone number for scheduling
  • Payment processors: For secure transaction processing (Razorpay, UPI)
  • Analytics providers: Google Analytics with anonymized data
  • Legal authorities: When required by law or court order

5Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission
  • Encrypted storage of sensitive information
  • Access controls — only authorized personnel can access customer data
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing

In the unlikely event of a data breach, we will notify affected users within 72 hours as required by applicable law.

6Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to restrict processing: Ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Right to object: Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Right to portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@fixmaster.in with the subject "Data Rights Request".

7Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will delete such information.

8Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Send an email notification to registered users
  • Display a prominent notice on our website

Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

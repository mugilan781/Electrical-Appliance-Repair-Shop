1Service Terms

FixMaster Appliance Repair ("FixMaster", "we", "us") provides electrical appliance repair services subject to these terms. By engaging our services, you agree to these conditions.

1.1 Scope of Services

  • Repair of electrical household appliances including fans, irons, mixer grinders, water heaters, and other small appliances
  • Preventive maintenance and annual maintenance contracts (AMC)
  • Home pickup and delivery logistics for repair items

1.2 Service Availability

Services are available in Chennai and select Tamil Nadu cities. Coverage areas are subject to change without prior notice. Emergency services are available 24/7 but may be subject to additional charges.

2Booking Policy

2.1 Booking Process

You may book a repair via our website, WhatsApp, or phone. All bookings are subject to technician availability and confirmation by FixMaster.

2.2 Pre-Repair Consent

Before any repair work begins, our technician will:

  • Diagnose the appliance and identify the issue
  • Provide a written or verbal cost estimate
  • Obtain your explicit approval before proceeding
No repair work is commenced without your explicit consent to the quoted cost. You may decline and pay only a ₹149 diagnostic fee.

2.3 Customer Obligations

  • Provide accurate contact and address information
  • Ensure the appliance is accessible at the scheduled time
  • Disclose any pre-existing damage or modifications
  • Remove personal data from smart appliances before service

3Warranty Terms

3.1 Standard Warranty

All repairs carry a 30-day warranty on the specific issue repaired. If the same problem recurs within 30 days, FixMaster will repair it free of charge.

3.2 Warranty Exclusions

The warranty does not cover:

  • New or different faults unrelated to the original repair
  • Damage caused by misuse, power surges, or accidents after repair
  • Appliances opened or tampered with by other technicians after our repair
  • Normal wear and tear of consumable parts

3.3 Spare Parts Warranty

Genuine OEM spare parts carry manufacturer warranties (typically 3–12 months depending on part type). Third-party equivalent parts carry a 30-day warranty from date of installation.

4Payment Terms

4.1 Payment Methods

  • Cash payment at service center or doorstep
  • UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM UPI)
  • Net banking and debit/credit cards
  • EMI options for AMC plans above ₹2,499

4.2 Payment Timing

Payment is due upon completion of repair and delivery of the appliance. For AMC plans, payment terms are as specified in the AMC agreement.

4.3 Invoicing

A GST-compliant invoice is provided for all repairs. Retain the invoice as it is required for warranty claims.

5Cancellation & Refunds

5.1 Cancellation Policy

  • Before pickup: Free cancellation with at least 2 hours notice
  • After pickup but before repair: ₹149 diagnostic fee applies
  • After repair commenced: Full quoted amount is payable

5.2 Refund Policy

Refunds are provided only in the following circumstances:

  • We are unable to fix the reported issue (no-fix, no-fee)
  • Damage caused directly by our technician during service
  • Overcharging beyond the agreed quotation

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.

FixMaster's total liability for any claim arising from our services shall not exceed the amount paid for the specific repair in question. We are not liable for:

  • Data loss from smart appliances during servicing

  • Force majeure events (natural disasters, power failures, etc.)
